## 1. One-paragraph contribution claim

> We release **NAICS-GH**, a corpus of **6,588 GitHub repositories** drawn from
> the **USA, European Union, and Australia** and labeled with 2-digit NAICS
> industry sector codes, covering **19 of the 20 sectors** (Sector 55,
> "Management of Companies and Enterprises", is absent because no repositories
> survived the score â‰¥ 8 filter under the 80-sample minimum). Labels are
> generated by **GPT-4.x** using a two-stage **retrieve-and-verify** pipeline:
> BGE-large-en embeddings + FAISS retrieval surface ~20 candidate
> repositories per NAICS subindustry, which are then scored by GPT against a
> structured rubric (1â€“10). Repositories scoring â‰¥ 8 are released as
> high-confidence labels. We validate the labels against a manually checked
> subset prepared by research assistants and demonstrate downstream utility by
> fine-tuning **RoBERTa-large** to predict NAICS sectors directly from
> repository name, description, topics, and README, reaching **86.45% F1 /
> 86.35% accuracy** on a held-out 20% test set. Dataset, code, prompts, and
> the fine-tuned model are released publicly.
